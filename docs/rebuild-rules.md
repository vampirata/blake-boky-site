# Rebuild rules (from Kait, 28 Oct 2026)

1. Visuals are 1:1 with the live Framer site: layout, type, colour, spacing, motion, at every breakpoint (Desktop >= 1400, Tablet 810 to 1399, Phone < 810).
2. Bugs and errors are fixed, not reproduced. Every fix is listed in `docs/fixes.md` so the client can see what changed.
3. Images are never cut off in a way that hides the artwork (Valentina's note). Galleries, cards and thumbnails show the whole piece.
4. Content and assets are the client's exact ones. Client copy is kept verbatim except obvious bugs (template leftovers, unfilled placeholders, wrong labels), which are fixed and listed.
5. No em or en dashes in any copy we write.

## Colour modes (Kait, 29 Oct 2026). This is a standing rule.

- **Blake = white/light mode.** Paper `#f5f2eb` (or `#f3f1e9` / `#f4f1ea` as measured), dark text `#1c1a17`, light nav. Blake's pages: Blake bio, Andromeda, Fragments, Coming Soon Blake, Blake-only news.
- **Boky = dark mode.** Ink `#0f0f0f`, light text `#f5f2eb` / `#dbdbdb`, grey nav. Boky's pages: Boky bio, Past Works (Boky's archive), In Lucem, Figurative Poetry, Coming Soon Boky, Boky-only news.
- **Blake & Boky together = grey mode.** Stone `#5c5852`, text `#dbdbdb` / `#f5f2eb`, black nav. Shared pages: Spirit Collection (their collaboration), the Collections index, News index and shared articles, and the home page's shared sections.
- **Exceptions: Exhibitions & Galleries and Contact stay in their original light colours** (paper / white, light nav). Kait, 29 Oct 2026: they look better that way.
- Gold `#e3caa6` / `#cca46c` is the accent in every mode.
- Any new page or CMS item takes its mode from whose work it is. If unclear, it is grey.

## Polish level

The site should feel Awwwards level (fluid, deliberate motion, refined detail) while keeping the client's layout, type and assets. Polish adds; it never cuts the artwork or changes the client's content.
